Ferrari 458 Italia — 3D model attribution
=========================================

The NOS download animation in this app draws a car sprite baked from a 3D model:

  "Ferrari 458 Italia" by vicent091036
  Distributed with the three.js examples as examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made for this app: the model was rendered offline to a flat 2D sprite
(512 px wide, transparent background), the paint colour was shifted to a cool
silver-blue, the wheels were removed (they are drawn live in the shader), and
the sprite is scaled, tinted and composited by the dock shader. The model is
not redistributed in 3D form; only the derived 2D sprite ships.
