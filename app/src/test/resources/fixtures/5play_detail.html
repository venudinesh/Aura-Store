About

Brawl Stars (MOD, Unlimited Money) runs on Null's Brawl, an unofficial private server with unlimited gems and coins. You can experiment with brawlers, upgrades and skins without the usual wait for currency. These resources belong to the private server, not your Supercell account; progress and matchmaking stay separate from the official game.

The fights are still fast and messy. In Gem Grab, hold ten gems while the other team tries to knock them out of your hands. Brawl Ball rewards a well-timed pass as much as a good shot, while Showdown leaves you collecting power cubes and watching the shrinking arena. Each brawler has a different attack range, Super and gadgets, so a close-range rush can go badly against someone who knows how to keep their distance.

Null's Brawl supports online matches with other people on its servers. Swap fighters to test an unfamiliar play style or build a team around one particular mode. A large currency balance gives you room to try things, but it doesn't transfer into the official Brawl Stars, and friends using the Google Play version won't appear in the same matches.
