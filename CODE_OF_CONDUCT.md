# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to talk about an Android app and the catalogues it reads. The
short version: be decent, argue about the work, and leave other people alone.

## What is expected

- Talk about the code, the design, the measurements and the sites. Bring evidence: a log line, a
  failure, a screenshot, a hash.
- Assume the other person is competent and has a reason. A parser that fails on one site is not
  the same as a parser that fails everywhere.
- Accept that a maintainer may hold a different view of a change. That is what the pull request is
  for.
- Keep the discussion in the open, in the tracker, where it can be read later.

## What is not

- Harassment, personal attacks, or anything aimed at who a person is rather than what they wrote.
- Sexual content, sexual attention, or anything of that kind involving anyone under 18.
- Threats, or instructions for doing harm to a person, a phone or a server.
- Publishing someone's private information: a name with an address, a phone number, a private
  key, an account or a token.
- Spam, or bulk reports, or an argument with a person instead of with their point.

## Reporting

If something happens that this document does not cover, open a private report through the
repository's Security tab, which says more about how that works in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or write to the maintainer through the repository's own page. Reports
are read by the maintainer, kept as private as the channel allows, and acted on.

This covers the repository, the issue tracker, the pull requests, and any space where someone is
taking part in the project.

The shape of this document follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), cut down
to what a project of this size needs.
